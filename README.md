### 🔬 Research Focus & Interests

My research sits at the intersection of **reliable autonomous systems** and **trustworthy medical AI**:

* **Continuous Authorization & Agentic Security (`PACA++`)**: Designing reference monitors and provenance-anchored authorization mechanisms for LLM agents and autonomous systems, ensuring actions remain bound to intent, policy, and untrusted-boundary verification rather than ungrounded model outputs.
* **Multimodal Clinical Disease Progression**: Longitudinal modeling of chronic disease trajectories integrating tabular electronic health records (EHR), medical imaging features, and continuous wearable sensor streams.
* **Algorithmic Fairness & Uncertainty**: Applying conformal prediction, calibrated uncertainty bounds, and fairness audits to clinical decision support systems.
